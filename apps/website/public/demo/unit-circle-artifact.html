Tip: drag anywhere on the circle picture — the angle snaps to your pointer (counter-clockwise from +x). Try quadrants I–IV, then creep up to 90° and 270° and watch tan explode. Math used: rad = deg·π/180, x = cos(rad), y = sin(rad), tan = sin/cos.
